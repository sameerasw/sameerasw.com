# MeDrop - NameDrop for Android
Share your contact with other easily with NFC or QR code.

# What's New?
- [MeDrop WearOS](https://github.com/sameerasw/MeDrop-wear)
- Ability to receive NFC scans
- Ability to customize profile contact name
- Quick settings tile prompt in settings
- Dynamic app shortcuts for profiles

- Fix QR code crash on large contact info profiles
- Fix profile photo not updating
